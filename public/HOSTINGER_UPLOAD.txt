Mama Math — Hostinger upload checklist
=====================================

Upload ALL files and folders from the dist/ folder into public_html
(do not upload the "dist" folder itself).

Required after upload:
1. Enable SSL (HTTPS) in Hostinger hPanel
2. In Supabase → Authentication → URL configuration, set Site URL to https://yourdomain.com
3. Add redirect URLs: https://yourdomain.com/** and https://yourdomain.com/auth/callback

Full guide: see HOSTINGER.md in the project repository.
